glpv — live examples

Every page below is one self-contained index.html written by glpv scan: the whole include and trigger graph of a GitLab CI setup, with the rules engine running in your browser so you can simulate any pipeline.

Five-project demoparent–child and multi-project triggers, includes across repos, a cycle, a dynamic child and an unresolved trigger — small enough to read at a glance. gitlab-org/gitlabGitLab's own CI: ~60 pipelines and ~3,000 jobs across four projects, scanned against a real diff. Try “stack children” and the search box.

Things to try in either map:

  • Change the pipeline source, ref or a variable in the sim bar — every job re-evaluates and downstream pipelines grey out when their trigger stops firing.
  • Click a job: the panel shows how it is invoked, its rule trace, and ⚡ Enable in simulation finds the inputs that would make it run.
  • Explore all possible outcomes enumerates every distinct way a job can end up.
  • Press / to search; copy link captures the simulation, selection and camera in the URL.
Source, releases and documentation: github.com/me1iissa/glpv. The gitlab-org map is rebuilt weekly from upstream's master.
